# *supcoda* v2.0

Welcome to *supcoda*, the new and updated walkthrough to supcode 6.0!

Here we’ll cover what supcode is all about, why it’s awesome and all the syntactical technicalities within.

Unlike something like the [Svelte tutorial](https://svelte.dev/tutorial) (which is insane, btw), *supcoda* is more of a rapid overview than a guided tutorial. Feel free to read the articles in any order, flit between them as needed, and stop once you’ve known enough to come back later and develop further.

> [!NOTE]
> *supcoda* presumes prior experience in coding!


## Portal

Start with the [introduction](core/README.md) to get a feel for supcode. Familiarise with the [core constructs](core/constructs.md), then explore the [individual topics](constructs/) however you like.

Full-stack 10x developer already? Lightning-quick rundown’s at [Learn X in Y Minutes, Where X = supcode](../learn.md)!
